Live order book, market depth and trades

ExchEngine Order Book shows the live order book, market depth and recent trades of a crypto pair on Binance and 11 other exchanges. Three linked views were built to reveal how price, volume and their distribution across the depth relate to each other, how far the price deviates, and how limit orders and walls press on it. Free, no sign-up, nothing to install.

Orders

A price ladder grouped by an adjustable step inside a chosen Δ% window around the price, with cumulative volume bars. A scale of bid versus ask shares shows the buying or selling pressure at a glance, and the volume can be read in coins, in the quote currency or converted to dollars.

Depth

Cumulative depth curves for both sides of the book, with drag, pinch zoom and inertia. The Δ% probe marks mirrored points on both sides of the price and compares the volume needed to move the price down and up by the same distance, so large walls and thin zones are easy to spot.

Trades

A live tape of recent trades coloured by side, for periods from 15 minutes to a day, to compare what actually traded with what is resting in the book.

Interface

Orders, Trades and Depth sit side by side on wide screens and in tabs on phones. The panels are drawn on a light canvas that works fast on old devices, Windows 7 and Android 7 included.
