Free online crypto chart with analysis tools

A fast candlestick chart for cryptocurrency pairs from Binance and ten other exchanges. No sign-up, no download; it works in any modern browser, including Windows 7 and Android 7.

Chart types

Candles, hollow candles, volume candles, bars, HLC bars, high-low, Heikin Ashi, line, line with markers, step line, area, HLC area, baseline, columns, Renko, line break, Kagi, range bars and point and figure. Growing and falling colours are adjustable.

Indicators and oscillators

SMA, EMA, WMA, Parabolic SAR, SuperTrend, Bollinger Bands, Donchian Channel, Envelope, VWAP, RSI, MACD, Stochastic, CCI, Williams %R, ROC, MFI, ADX, ATR, OBV and Awesome Oscillator, with adjustable periods and colours.

Drawing and analysis tools

Trend lines, horizontal and vertical lines, Fibonacci retracement levels, Elliott impulse waves 1-2-3-4-5 and corrective waves A-B-C. Drawings are stored in your browser, separately for each pair.

Timeframes and history

Timeframes from 1 minute to 1 week, a logarithmic price scale, an inverted scale, and scrolling back to the very first candle the exchange recorded.

Wide range of chart types and markup

Nineteen chart types, from candles and Heikin Ashi to Renko, Kagi, line break and point and figure, plus Fibonacci levels, Elliott waves, trend lines and other markup tools placed right on the chart.

Indicators and oscillators

Moving averages, bands and channels, SuperTrend, Parabolic SAR, VWAP and oscillators such as RSI, MACD, Stochastic, CCI, ADX and OBV, each with adjustable periods and colours.

Flexible tools, import and export

Add as many instruments as you need, in any combination and order, and keep them separately for every pair. Export your markup and indicators to a text or file and import them back on any device.

Write your own indicators

Create your own indicators and analysis tools in a built-in formula language: parameters, moving averages, conditions, lines, histograms, levels, and panes on the price or under it. Formulas are evaluated safely inside the page, with no sign-up and nothing to install.

Interface and logo

A light canvas chart with touch, pinch and mouse control that stays fast on old devices, Windows 7 and Android 7 included. The ExchEngine logo, a gear with the letter e, is shown on wide screens.
